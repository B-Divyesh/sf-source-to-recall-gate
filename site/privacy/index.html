How your study data stays private

Your passages, answers, and exports stay on your device. The product has no account system.

What the product stores

The web app stores passages, source details, answers, and export dates in this browser. The extension uses its own browser storage.

A Press Pass token and its last check are stored locally after activation. Demo data uses separate keys beginning with demo:.

What the product sends

Study content never leaves your device. License checks send only the license token to the Sociobot billing API over HTTPS.

Sociobot and Dodo handle checkout details. This site loads no outside fonts, scripts, ads, analytics, or trackers.

Browser permissions

The extension stores your passages and adds a selection menu. It reads selected text only after your capture action.

Access to api.sociobot.in only checks Press Pass tokens. The extension does not scrape the rest of a page.

Delete or export data

Choose Delete all local data to erase study data in this browser. Removing the extension also removes its local data.

Individual exports are free. Press Pass holders can download a complete local backup.
